# Accepted — D03 improvement 3

The narrow yellow leading edge adds warm/cool contrast and a clear shoreline.
It follows the whole curve without detached smears or gaps. White negative space,
aqua band and white surf/blue section remain legible. Against c02, the composition
has a stronger leading contour and less uniform pastel colour. The width varies
with curve slope because the masks are horizontal offsets, not normal offsets;
this remains a smooth deliberate ribbon, not broken residue.
