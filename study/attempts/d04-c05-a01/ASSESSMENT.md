# Accepted — D04 improvement 5

Compared with c04, upper and middle bands are substantially finer and separated
by more black space. The stepped pattern feels less bulky and more delicate;
the two tapered accents remain intact. The lower curve retains a stronger
crescent profile, so do not claim equal line weights or that all three changed
equally. Smooth contours and readable hierarchy are preserved, without the
rounded bite failure of the radial-slot experiment.

This is the fifth accepted improvement for this idea. Glaze next and then use
a completely new concept. The result is a stylised nested-arc ornament, not a
literal ribbed hand fan or a claim of metallic polish.
