# D02 baseline — Scarlet camellia botanical

Clean opaque white base verified after Alex's correction. The red five-petal
bloom is legible and its sage stem/leaf pair gives a genuinely botanical concept,
not a celestial variation. This is a considered baseline, not an accepted cycle.

Main weaknesses: the yellow centre is almost the size of each petal and flattens
the bloom into a badge; the stem is too broad and the round-ended leaf branches
look heavy. The flower is pleasing as a simple graphic but not yet a refined
botanical design. Briar's assessment is not Alex's approval.

First hypothesis: repaint the identical five red petal contacts over the yellow
centre. Their overlaps should leave a smaller warm central shape while preserving
the original outer petal silhouette. Then address plant weight/leaf articulation.
Glaze only after five accepted improvements. Baseline video/images and complete
replay chain (clean base followed by all motif stages) are retained.
