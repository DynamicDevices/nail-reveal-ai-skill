# Accepted — D14 improvement 4

The shaft now ends in an open, continuous left-facing J hook. This makes the
umbrella recognisable rather than mushroom-like and balances the long vertical.
The black interior stays open; the outer curve is clean and detached gold
fragments are absent. The inner join is angular, not a precision circular wire.
Its bold weight is consistent with the shaft. Visual judgement: Briar.
