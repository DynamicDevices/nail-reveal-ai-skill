# Accepted — D16 improvement 3

Four round black spots turn the generic shell into a recognisable ladybird.
Each remains separated from the central seam,from its neighbour and from the
outer white field in closeup and Hand view. Both rows stay inside the red body;
no sidewall clipping or merged black patch. Large clean spots deliberately suit
the tested broad brush rather than imitating fine real insect markings. Briar.
