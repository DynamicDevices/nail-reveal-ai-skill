# Accepted — D15 improvement 1

neck/rim. Scarlet and pale gold now have a clear material boundary; white margins
are clear. The cap is bold and rounded, not thin metal. Its width is contained
within the body's widest span. This establishes structure rather than arbitrary
decoration, but the object is not yet a finished lantern. Visual judgement:Briar.
