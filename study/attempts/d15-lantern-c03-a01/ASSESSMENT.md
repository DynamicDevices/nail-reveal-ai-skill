# Accepted — D15 improvement 3

make its rounded volume read as a paper lantern rather than a capped oval.

clear. Both caps reconnect to the ribs after their exact replay. The ribs are
graphic broad curves, not physically measured constant-width wire. This is a
substantial change in material/readability and preserves the balanced frame.
