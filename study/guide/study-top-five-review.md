Selection by Briar from all20 recorded glazed Hand views,followed by original
closeup inspection of the shortlist. This is an aesthetic judgement,not Alex's
endorsement,a game score,or a claim that every study outcome is equally strong.

Evidence: [all20 glazed designs](study-twenty-glazed-designs.png) and
[shortlist closeups](study-shortlist-closeups.png). Both retain source/crop maps.

## Scope of the next trials

Alex requested each selected design on EVERY nail of a full hand: five complete
sets,not five different accent nails on one hand. No set has been executed yet.
Record creation,per-nail checks and each glazed full-hand result. Keep adaptations
and failures reproducible; these are not additional accepted study-cycle counts.
Before phone execution,verify existing signed game-test coverage for this phase,
the same subject/host/device,expiry and exclusions. A plan is not authority.

## What the overview does not prove

The ledger reaches100 inspected changes across20 ideas. Several baselines were
unfinished material and several changes were construction steps; this is not
proof that100 already-finished designs were independently optimised. The count
also cannot certify beauty. The weaker prototypes must remain in the report.

The overview raises specific remaining quality questions: D07 wing-edge/glint
clarity,D11 ribbon-edge finish,and D12 recognisability at hand scale. Inspect
their original Hand/closeup evidence before claiming P0 met for every idea or
deciding whether a historical acceptance needs withdrawing. Don't infer clipping
or count a rejection from a small contact sheet alone. D04's fan is a very simple
glyph; D14 and D18 are more schematic than the selected five. None should be
presented as equally brilliant just because it has five ledger entries.

Remaining delivery: final quality audit,the five complete-hand trials,privacy
review,reproducible media/instructions,portable skill/README updates,and reviewed
development-branch publication. No merge,social launch or finished-project claim.
