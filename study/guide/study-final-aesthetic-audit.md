# Final aesthetic audit — before full-hand trials

Original glazed closeups for D07, D11 and D12 were inspected, not just thumbnails.
The full twenty-design Hand comparison and selected-five closeups were also
inspected. This is Briar's visual assessment, not Alex's approval or an
independent aesthetic review.

D07 has a clean black field and separate teal wing shapes, but its disconnected
forms and oversized pearl make it read more as an abstract brooch than a
convincing butterfly. The broad white wing highlights are heavy. It is not a
top-five design.

D11 reads clearly as an ivory bow on plum. The loop slits differ in width and
the broad tails are stylised. The apparent thumbnail roughness is not proof of
stray polish: the original shows intentional cuts and a clean surrounding field.

D12 has a clean teal surround and a continuous fish-like white silhouette,
but the large red/black discs dominate. Koi recognisability is weak. It is a
useful recorded limitation, not a brilliant finished koi illustration.

These observations do not establish that an individual earlier accepted change
was worse than its parent, so no acceptance is silently withdrawn here. They do
mean the 100-entry ledger cannot certify beauty P0. Many steps constructed
unfinished designs rather than independently optimising finished designs.

The strongest candidates remain D03, D05, D20, D08 and D06. Full-hand execution
and per-nail adaptation must be judged from actual game captures; repeating
screen coordinates is not proof that a design fits another nail.
