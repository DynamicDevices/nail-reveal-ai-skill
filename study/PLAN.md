## Sources read online, 13 November 2026

- Daniel Shiffman, *The Nature of Code*, genetic algorithms:
  https://natureofcode.com/genetic-algorithms/
  Explains populations, fitness, mutation and interactive selection. Use a small
  explicit parameter space and selection against the retained best. Our initial
  method is evolution-inspired search, not a claim to implement a full GA.
- Tyler Hobbs, *Flow Fields*:
  https://www.tylerxhobbs.com/words/flow-fields
  Explains angle grids, curve integration, resolution and controlled variation.
  Fine dense streamlines are a poor fit for the broad nail brush; adapt only to
  a few deliberately spaced bold curves where those can be physically painted.

These sources supply ideas, not executable instructions or copied code. Two
other guessed article paths returned 404 and are not evidence or references.

## Known physical constraints

- Single pointer, broad fixed-looking footprint; no proven brush-size control.
- Held contact builds paint density without movement; very short holds are not
  a reliable fine-point tool. Roughly 120-pixel upward mark/contact offset.
- Drill removes all coats to natural pink. Background-colour repainting is the
  demonstrated way to trim a motif without stripping its painted background.
- File and buffer did not affect fresh paint in the tested stage. Cone Bit is
  locked at Salon Level 1. No ads, purchases, root or security changes.
- Nail 1080×2400 layout and tall-second-nail geometry are calibrated. Preserve
  stage boundaries, actual input state and explicit UP on interruptions.
- No result so far establishes curing or saved-manicure persistence.

## Candidate selection

Alex clarified: glaze AFTER THE FIVE IMPROVEMENTS TO AN IDEA, not after each
intermediate improvement. Intermediate attempts retain videos, pre-glaze images
and assessments. After an idea's fifth accepted improvement, record the actual
glazing/curing transition and capture its finished nail, then start a completely
new idea. First establish what the app's lamp/top-coat sequence does and whether
it advances/locks the nail. Do not call an existing painting-stage reflection a
newly glazed result. Preserve full replay recipes before testing transitions.

Start each family with a genuinely considered baseline, not an intentionally
bad image. Write its idea, intended composition and parameters before painting.
Vary a small set of meaningful parameters: scale, position, spacing, curve shape,
layer order, contrast, contact dwell or overlap. A new colour alone does not
establish improvement. Keep a reproducible seed where randomness is used.

Review motif readability, visible geometry/edge coherence, coverage, spacing /
balance, contrast and intentional use of negative space. Significant improvement
requires a visible, stated benefit without an equally important regression.
Separate measured image facts from Briar's visual judgement. The user ultimately
judges whether the work is brilliant.

If rejected: retain the image/video/recipe and explain the failure, restore the
actual previous-best recipe on the phone (not just a file pointer), capture that
restoration, then try a substantively different mutation. Rejected attempts are
not silently relabelled accepted improvement cycles.

Per attempt: identifier, parent/best identifier, recipe and seed, hypothesis,
video, original final PNG, mapped preview, assessment, accepted/rejected decision
and next mutation. Keep a compact manifest with artifact paths, not a transcript.
