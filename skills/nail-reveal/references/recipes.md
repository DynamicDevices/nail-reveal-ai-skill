# Recorded painting recipe

For the subsequent continuous-contact experiment, see the
[recorded UK-flag recipe](uk-flag.md). It is stylised, not a precise flag rendering.

Tested on Nail Reveal 1.25, Android 15, 1080×2400, the tall second nail
in Zara's two-nail repair. These are physical coordinates. Establish the
same screen state before replaying; do not blindly reuse on another layout.

Preconditions: preparation stages complete, painting selected, tall second
nail selected, colour palette open and settled, recorder confirmed running.
The real test began with the palette already open; this distinction matters.

Blue selection: tap 540,1415; wait 0.7 s; close at 540,1740; wait 0.7 s.
Paint 280,640 → 280,1870 over 1800 ms, then
390,530 → 390,1970 over 1800 ms.

White selection: open Colours at 230,2200; wait 0.7 s;
tap 390,1560; wait 0.7 s; close at 540,1740; wait 0.7 s.
Paint 510,480 → 510,2010 over 2000 ms, then
610,480 → 610,2010 over 2000 ms.

Red selection: open Colours at 230,2200; wait 0.7 s;
tap 840,1115; wait 0.7 s; close at 540,1740; wait 0.7 s.
Paint 740,530 → 740,1970 over 1800 ms, then
850,650 → 850,1870 over 1800 ms.

Observed: distinct blue, white and red vertical regions. Broad soft boundaries,
rounded stroke ends and an unpainted lower rim remain. Do not describe this as
a perfectly covered or cured flag. Screenshot retained privately.

For the short first nail, client-colour selection plus six vertical strokes
at x=280,400,520,640,760,850, lasting 1600 ms each and following the silhouette,
made a recognisable single-colour coat and the card said the nail was done.
Its visible lower rim still needed work. The affected-nail right arrow wrapped
from the second nail back to the first in this observed two-nail task.
